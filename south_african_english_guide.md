# Cannibisters Style Guide - South African English

## Spelling Standards

**Use South African English spelling throughout all product descriptions and metafields.**

### Common Differences from American English

- **Flavours** (not flavors)
- **Colour** (not color)
- **Honour** (not honor)
- **Favourite** (not favorite)
- **Recognise** (not recognize)
- **Organised** (not organized)
- **Centre** (not center)
- **Metre** (not meter)
- **Litre** (not liter)

### Product Description Guidelines

When creating product descriptions:
- Use South African spelling consistently
- Maintain the premium, evocative tone
- Keep descriptions concise (2-3 sentences)
- Focus on flavour, effects, and use case

### Example (Correct South African English)

> "Orange Cake is an energising sativa-dominant hybrid bursting with sweet orange, vanilla cake, and citrus zest flavours. Expect an uplifting, euphoric high that sparks creativity and social energy—perfect for daytime adventures and mood elevation."

### Common Cannibisters Vocabulary

- **Flavours** - always use the SA spelling
- **Energising** (not energizing)
- **Emphasise** (not emphasize)
- **Realise** (not realize)
- **Characterise** (not characterize)

---

**Note:** This applies to all product content, marketing materials, and customer-facing text.
