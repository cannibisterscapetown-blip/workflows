# 🤖 Cannibisters Agent Handbook

*For all staff. Pin this (or the per-channel canvas) in every agent channel.*

We have a team of AI agents in Slack. Each one has a job, a channel, and
limits. Tag the agent in its channel, say what you need in one message,
and it replies in the thread. A human always stays in charge of publishing,
sending, and anything outside policy.

## Who to tag

### 🌿 Strain Agent — `#agent-strains`
**For:** new strains or products that landed at HQ and need their Shopify
listing completed (description, metafields, tags).

> @Claude new strain: **Peach Jam**, flower, loaded by Martine this morning

- It fills the listing in our house style and posts the full breakdown for review.
- It **never publishes** — someone at HQ checks the thread and flips the
  product to Active.
- Include what you know (breeder, THC, photos); it researches the rest.

### 💳 Loyalty Agent — `#agent-loyalty` *(private channel)*
**For:** member loyalty questions and discount fixes while you're at the budbar.

> @Claude what's the loyalty status and discount for member jane@example.com?
> @Claude Sipho M (member #4521) should be on Gold — his 10% isn't applying

- Give it one way to identify the member: email, phone, or member number.
- It can change a discount **within policy limits**; bigger changes get
  posted for a manager to ✅ first.
- It tells *you* the member's info — you relay it to the member. Don't
  screenshot the thread to customers.
- Disputes ("I was promised 15%") → it gives you the facts, a manager decides.

### 📣 Marketing Agent — `#agent-marketing`
**For:** campaign stats, email drafts, ad checks, monthly reports.

> @Claude how did last week's new-drop email perform?
> @Claude draft the Friday drop email for these 3 strains: …

- Drafts only — a human always presses send on emails and ad changes.

### ❓ Not sure? — `#agent-help`
Tag the agent there, describe what you need, and it points you to the
right channel with an example message.

## House rules

1. **One task per thread.** New task = new tag, new thread.
2. **Say it all in one message** — who/what/why. The agents answer once;
   drip-feeding details costs everyone time.
3. **Check the receipt.** After any change, the agent posts what it changed
   (old → new). If a receipt looks wrong, reply in the thread and tag a manager.
4. **Agents can say no.** "That's outside my policy, tagging a manager" is
   the agent working correctly — don't try to talk it around.
5. **Something it should be able to do but can't?** Tell the owner —
   agents learn by having their job description updated, not by being
   argued with in Slack.
