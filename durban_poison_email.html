Durban Poison

South Africa's Finest Landrace Sativa

Pure Sativa · Indoor · R240 per gram

Durban Poison is not just a strain — it's a living piece of South African heritage. One of the oldest surviving cannabis landraces on earth, it was cultivated in the hills outside Durban long before the modern seed industry existed, shaped by the soil, sun and culture of our continent over hundreds of years.

Where most modern strains are engineered hybrids, Durban Poison is something rarer: a pure expression. No crossing, no tampering — just a proud, unadulterated sativa that delivers the kind of clean, soaring energy you simply cannot manufacture. Expect a sharp, anise-sweet aroma, electric cerebral clarity, and a creativity boost that's earned this strain a global cult following.

We don't always have it. When we do, it moves fast. This is your window — don't sleep on it.
