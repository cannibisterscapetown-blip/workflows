## Key Compliance Rules (Read First)

Meta/Instagram still restricts cannabis product promotion globally. To keep the account safe:

- **Never show consumption** (no product touching lips/mouth)
- **Describe herbs by flavour/effect profile** — not strain names or THC% in captions
- **No pricing in posts** — direct that to DMs, your website, or WhatsApp
- **Avoid explicit hashtags** like #cannabis #weed #420 — use coded alternatives: #herb #plant #apothecary #wellness #greenlife
- **Use coded language** in captions: "herb," "the plant," "the good stuff," "green," "flower"
- **Keep a backup account** — cannabis accounts get flagged randomly. Post there occasionally so you can recover fast if your main gets suspended. Save all content to cloud storage.
- **Add closed captions** — many people scroll without sound

## The 10 Content Ideas

### 1. "POV: You just walked into Cannibisters for the first time"
**Format:** POV Reel | 15–30 seconds
**Trending mechanic:** First-person immersive walk-through

**The Skit:**
Staff member holds the phone like it's the customer's eyes — walking through the front door, scanning the beautiful apothecary aesthetic, past the herb display, the coffee counter, to the lounge. Use slow, cinematic movement with a luxurious trending audio track.

**What to film:**
- Start outside the front door, push it open
- Pan slowly across the shelves and product displays
- Show the coffee station
- End on the cosy lounge area

**Caption angle:** *"This is not your average herbal experience. Welcome home. 🌿"*

**Why it works:** POV reels dominate the algorithm right now. Sells the experience without showing product — aspirational, not promotional. Drives curiosity and foot traffic.

---

### 2. "What people think a cannabis club looks like vs. Cannibisters"
**Format:** Split-screen or back-to-back Reel | 20–30 seconds
**Trending mechanic:** Expectation vs. Reality

**The Skit:**
- First clip: Dark, dingy, exaggerated "old school" vibe — maybe a staff member in a hoodie in a dim corner looking sketchy, dramatic music
- Second clip (after beat drop): Cannibisters in full glory — warm lighting, beautiful shelves, barista making coffee, staff in clean attire, sunlit lounge

**What to film:**
- A quick, overdramatic "dodgy" setup (comedic, not realistic)
- Then a beautiful golden-hour walkthrough of the real store

**Caption angle:** *"We're changing the narrative, one cup of infused coffee at a time ☕🌿"*

**Why it works:** Directly fights the stigma. Positions Cannibisters as premium and lifestyle-forward. Highly shareable — people will tag friends.

### 3. "Day in the Life: Opening the Apothecary"
**Format:** Day-in-the-life Reel | 30–60 seconds
**Trending mechanic:** Behind-the-scenes / staff lifestyle

**The Skit:**
Follow one staff member from the moment they arrive. Use timestamp text overlays:

- **7:45AM** — Keys in the door, lights flicking on
- **8:00AM** — Arranging the product display, restocking shelves
- **8:15AM** — Coffee machine warming up, barista setup
- **8:30AM** — Quick team huddle / morning chat
- **9:00AM** — First member of the day walks in, warm greeting

Use an ambient or trending chill morning audio track underneath.

**Caption angle:** *"Another day in the most unique 'office' in Sea Point. ☀️🌿"*

**Why it works:** Authenticity is king in 2026. Members and potential members feel genuinely connected to the brand. The store's aesthetic does all the visual heavy lifting.

### 4. "Meet the Team" Staff Spotlight Series
**Format:** Quick-cut intro Reel | 20–30 seconds each (run as an ongoing series)
**Trending mechanic:** "Let me introduce myself" + fun facts format

**The Skit:**
Each team member gets their own reel. Staff walks into frame confidently with trending audio, then text overlays appear one by one:

- Name + role + how long at Cannibisters
- Favourite terpene or aroma profile (described by flavour, not strain name)
- A weird talent or random fun fact
- Something they love about the members

**What to film:**
Each staff member in their natural spot in the store — barista at the machine, floor staff by the shelves, etc.

**Caption angle:** *"This is [Name]. Ask them anything about terpenes. We dare you. 🌿"*

**Why it works:** People buy from people. Humanises the brand. Builds loyalty and makes members feel at home. Each staff member shares it to their personal following — free reach every time.

### 5. "Things only Cannibisters members will understand"
**Format:** List-style comedy Reel | 30–45 seconds
**Trending mechanic:** Relatable insider content + humour

**The Skit:**
Staff member (or a series of staff) acts out relatable member moments with text overlays:

- *"When someone asks 'what do you recommend?' and you genuinely don't know where to start"* — staff looks hilariously overwhelmed
- *"When a member brings in their friend for the first time and you watch them take it all in"* — proud, knowing smile
- *"When the barista already knows your order before you say anything"* — knowing nod
- *"When someone tries to describe what they're looking for and it gets very... specific"* — staff nodding super seriously with a poker face
- *"27,000 members and it somehow still feels like your local"* — warm smile to camera

**Caption angle:** *"You know who you are. 😏🌿 Tag your Cannibisters person."*

**Why it works:** Inside-joke content creates massive community feeling. Members share it because it's literally about them. Strong word-of-mouth engine for new memberships.

### 6. "Herb Sommelier" — Flavour Profile Explainer Series
**Format:** Educational Reel | 30–45 seconds (run as an ongoing series)
**Trending mechanic:** "Did you know?" + parody expert format

**The Skit:**
Staff member stands at the display, picks up a jar or product (label not visible), and talks about it exactly like a wine sommelier — dramatic swirl, exaggerated sniff, over-the-top descriptors:

- *"Notes of citrus and fresh pine... a hint of earthiness on the finish... with a bright, uplifting aromatic profile..."*
- Use cinematic close-ups of the jar, the packaging
- Staff delivers the tasting notes with complete deadpan seriousness, then breaks into a grin at the end

Frame it entirely around: flavour profiles, terpene education, aromatic notes. Never mention strain names, THC%, or pricing.

**Caption angle:** *"At Cannibisters, we take our herbs very seriously. 🌿🍷 What's your flavour?"*

**Why it works:** Funny, educational, genuinely unique to the brand. The sommelier angle is trending across food/beverage content and hasn't been done well in the SA cannabis retail space. Makes your staff look like genuine experts.

### 7. "Customer Type Bingo" — Staff Comedy Skit
**Format:** Character comedy skit | 45–60 seconds
**Trending mechanic:** "Types of [people]" viral format + bingo card graphic

**The Skit:**
One or two staff members play (lovingly, not mockingly) different member archetypes:

- **The Regular** — walks in, already at the counter before the door is closed, barely needs to speak
- **The First-Timer** — wide-eyed, reads every single label, asks 10 questions, bless them
- **The Researcher** — arrives with notes, asks specifically about linalool vs. myrcene ratios
- **The Chiller** — just here for the lounge and the infused coffee, not in a rush, not today
- **The Gifter** — "It's for my friend's birthday, they like... things that are calm? But also fun?"

Use a bingo card graphic overlay as each type gets "called."

**Caption angle:** *"Which one are you? Drop your number in the comments 😂🌿"*

**Why it works:** Tag-a-friend content = free viral reach. Funny, relatable, gets members engaging in comments. South Africans love this style of warm, observational humour.

### 8. Cape Town Lifestyle Collab Reel — Local Pride
**Format:** Location/lifestyle montage | 30–45 seconds
**Trending mechanic:** City identity + lifestyle aesthetic

**The Skit:**
Film a series of beautiful Cape Town lifestyle shots woven together with shots inside Cannibisters — no cannabis product needs to be visible:

- Signal Hill or Lion's Head sunrise → staff unlocking the store
- Sea Point promenade morning walk → arriving at Cannibisters
- Ocean view → cosy Cannibisters lounge interior
- Table Mountain → barista crafting a cup of infused coffee
- Colourful Bo-Kaap → warm product shelf display

Use a cinematic Cape Town track or a trending feel-good audio. End on the Cannibisters signage or a smiling staff member.

**Caption angle:** *"Cape Town's favourite ritual. Morning, noon, or whenever you need it. 🌊🏔️🌿"*

**Why it works:** Taps into Cape Town identity and local pride — massively shareable by locals. Attracts tourists discovering the city's cannabis scene. Zero compliance risk. Connects to a wider lifestyle audience beyond existing members.

### 9. "That Friday Feeling at Cannibisters" — Energy Reel
**Format:** Fast-cut hype Reel | 15–20 seconds
**Trending mechanic:** Weekend energy / trending upbeat audio

**The Skit:**
Film the store at peak Friday afternoon energy:

- Members arriving, smiling, greeting staff
- Barista working fast, steam rising from the machine
- Staff greeting regulars by name
- Lounge full and buzzing
- Quick cuts timed tightly to a trending beat drop

End on a staff member looking directly at camera with a subtle, confident nod.

**Caption angle:** *"You already know where to be. 🌿✌️ Friday vibes at the Apothecary."*

**Pro tip:** Post this Thursday evening or Friday morning to drive actual foot traffic the same day.

**Why it works:** Energy is contagious. Shows the store is alive and social. Triggers serious FOMO. Works as a recurring Friday series — same format, fresh energy each week.

### 10. "Things We Wish Every Member Knew" — Honest Staff Takes
**Format:** Talking-head / honest list Reel | 30–45 seconds
**Trending mechanic:** "Honest thoughts" / insider knowledge viral format

**The Skit:**
One warm, genuine staff member sits or stands in front of the camera and shares real tips — friendly and helpful, not salesy:

- *"You don't need to know anything when you walk in. That's literally what we're here for. Just tell us how you want to feel."*
- *"Our barista will actually change your life. Don't skip the infused coffee."*
- *"Membership is R100 a month. You'll make that back on your first visit easy."*
- *"It's completely okay to take your time. Nobody is rushing you."*
- *"We have over 90 options. It sounds overwhelming, but we've got you — just start with one question."*

Keep the tone warm, personal, and completely genuine. No script energy.

**Caption angle:** *"Straight from the team. We just want your visit to be perfect. 🌿"*

**Why it works:** "Insider knowledge" content builds massive trust and authenticity. Drives new member sign-ups organically by removing intimidation. Performs exceptionally well for service businesses where knowing what to expect matters.

## Bonus: Audio Strategy

The single biggest reach multiplier on Reels is **trending audio**. Here's how to find it:

1. Open Instagram → tap "+" to create a Reel → tap the music icon → select "Trending"
2. Look for the **upward arrow** icon next to a track — that means it's gaining momentum right now
3. **Sweet spot:** Sounds with under 5,000 Reels already using them. Post within 24–48 hours of spotting a rising sound.
4. Check TikTok too — sounds go viral there 3–7 days before they peak on Instagram.

You don't need cannabis-related audio. The visuals and text overlays do the storytelling. The trending audio is just the vehicle.

## Lifestyle Themes That Pair Naturally with Your Content

These angles extend your reach beyond existing members without triggering compliance issues:

- **Sleep & Recovery** — frame certain herb profiles as part of a bedtime ritual
- **Mindfulness** — cannabis as a tool for presence and slowing down
- **Soft Life / Self-Care** — warm, cosy, luxurious aesthetics resonate deeply with SA audiences
- **Creative Flow** — the connection between certain herb profiles and creative output
- **Cape Town Lifestyle** — the city itself is your backdrop and your brand asset
- **Conscious Consumption** — informed, intentional adult use. You know your terpenes, you choose deliberately.
