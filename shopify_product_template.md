## Field Guidelines

### Lineage
- Format: Parent 1 x Parent 2
- Add brief context in parentheses if helpful
- Example: "Ice Cream Cake x Whipped Cream (dessert-gas hybrid lineage; widely accepted cut)"

### Flowering Time
- Use week ranges: "8–9 weeks" or "9–10 weeks"
- Use en-dash (–) not hyphen (-)

### Profile
- Format: XX/XX [Dominant Type]
- Examples: "70/30 Indica-Dominant Hybrid", "60/40 Sativa-Dominant Hybrid", "50/50 Balanced Hybrid"

### Height
- Options: Short, Medium, Tall
- Can add context: "Medium-Tall" if between categories

### Stretch Percentage
- Format: ±XX%
- Typical ranges: 20-50%
- Use ± symbol

### Average THC Levels
- Format: XX–XX%
- Use en-dash (–) not hyphen (-)
- Provide realistic ranges based on testing/breeder info

### Flavours
- List 3-5 dominant flavours
- Separate with commas
- Order from most to least prominent
- Common descriptors: cream, vanilla, gas, citrus, pine, earth, berry, sweet, spice, diesel, fruity, floral

### Effects
- List 4-6 effects
- Separate with bullet points (•)
- Common effects: Relaxed, Euphoric, Happy, Creative, Focused, Uplifted, Energetic, Calm, Dreamy, Giggly, Sleepy, Hungry

### Breeder
- Name the breeder/genetics company
- Add context in parentheses if needed
- Example: "Exotic Genetix (commonly credited lineage source)"

### Used for
- List 2-4 use cases
- Separate with commas
- Examples: Evening relaxation, creative sessions, social gatherings, pain relief, sleep aid, daytime focus, appetite stimulation

### Plant Characteristics
- List 3-4 physical/aromatic traits
- Separate with bullet points (•)
- Examples: Dense frosty buds, Sweet dessert aroma, Indica-leaning structure, Purple hues, Resinous coating

### Plant Class
- Format: [Type]-dominant Flowering Plant
- Examples: "Indica-dominant Flowering Plant", "Sativa-dominant Flowering Plant", "Balanced Hybrid Flowering Plant"

### Suitable Space
- Options: Indoor, Outdoor, Greenhouse, Indoor or Greenhouse, Indoor or Outdoor
- Choose based on strain characteristics

### Short Description
- 2-3 sentences maximum
- Structure: [Flavor profile] + [Effects] + [Ideal use]
- Maintain Cannibisters' evocative, premium tone
- Avoid technical jargon
- Focus on experience

## Style Guidelines

### Tone
- Premium but approachable
- Evocative and sensory
- Educational without being clinical
- Enthusiastic about quality

### Language
- Use vivid flavour descriptors
- Emphasize experience and effects
- Keep it concise
- Avoid overly technical terms

### Formatting
- Use bullet points (•) for lists within fields
- Use commas for simple lists
- Maintain consistent capitalization
- Use proper en-dashes (–) not hyphens (-) for ranges

### Consistency
- Match the style of previous entries
- Use established vocabulary
- Maintain field order
- Keep formatting uniform
