Digital Marketing Performance Report – December 2025

Executive Summary / Key Insights

December's performance was defined by efficiency and intent. Despite technical challenges that restricted our Meta Ads presence, we generated significantly more leads. Our email strategy shifted to quality over quantity, driving more revenue with fewer sends. Local interest spiked with the summer season, driving record calls to the store.

  • Meta Efficiency Win: Despite a 35% drop in spend due to a 2-week account lockout, we generated 41% MORE leads. This proves the underlying strategy is incredibly robust.
  • Email Quality Over Quantity: We sent 57% fewer emails but achieved a 58% higher open rate and 11% more revenue. The list is healthier and more responsive.
  • Local Summer Surge: Google My Business saw a 49% increase in phone calls and 17% more direction requests, signaling strong physical demand during the festive season.
  • Google Ads Traffic: Clicks increased by nearly 60%, driving traffic to the new site. (Note: Conversion tracking is currently disabled on the temporary landing page).

What Worked / What Didn’t

What Worked
  • Meta Lead Gen: Massive efficiency gain. More leads for 35% less cost is a huge win.
  • Email Segmentation: Sending fewer, better emails proved that we don't need to spam to sell.
  • Local Intent: The GMB listing captured the summer tourist/local rush effectively.
What Didn’t
  • Ad Account Access: The 2-week lockout was a critical operational failure. I have added my personal Facebook account (Norton's) as an admin of the Ad account to prevent this from happening again.
  • Instagram Engagement: A steep drop-off from November's video viral success; need to reignite consistent content.

Opportunities & Recommendations

  1. Fix Tracking ASAP: Implement conversion tracking on the co.za domain to properly measure the value of the 60% increase in Google Ads traffic.
  2. Scale Meta Back Up: Now that access is restored and we know the CPA is excellent, we should aggressively return to full budget to capitalize on the high lead conversion rate.
  3. Maintain Email Quality: Continue the targeted, lower-volume email strategy as it is driving higher revenue per email sent.
  4. Focus on Webapp Growth: We are shifting the focus of all Google & Meta ads to drive traffic directly to the webapp (www.cannibisters.co.za). This strategic move is designed to capture new and existing users and drive signups for the app.
