## Key Insights

### 🏆 Top Performers
- **Brainstorm Joint (Dec 21):** Highest customer engagement with **25 separate orders**.
- **Queen of the South (Dec 02):** Most volume moved with **38 units**.
- **Lemon Frost (Dec 22):** Strong performance with **23 units** across 8 orders.

### 📉 Low Engagement Areas
- **High-Ticket Items:** Products like Gift Jars (Beginner, Advanced, Master) and premium items like Hash Rosin or Rosin Moonsticks saw 0 sales on their featured days. This suggests that the 20% discount may not have been enough to trigger impulse purchases for these higher-priced products during the advent period.
- **Vape Consistency:** While the Grand Daddy vape sold 1 unit, the Green Crack vape saw no sales on its day.

### 💡 Recommendation
For future campaigns, consider placing high-performing lower-priced items (like joints or chocolates) on weekdays to maintain momentum, and perhaps offering a deeper flash discount for the premium gift jars on key dates (like Dec 1 or Dec 24) to improve conversion.
