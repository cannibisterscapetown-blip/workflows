# Digital Marketing Performance Report – November 2025

## **Executive Summary / Key Insights**

November was a strong month for engagement and reach across the board, with Meta and Instagram driving significant visibility. While we saw a dip in Google Ads conversion efficiency, the volume of impressions and interactions remains high.

*   **Meta Reach & Engagement Exploded:** A massive **49.7% increase in impressions** and **46.6% increase in reach** on Meta Ads shows our creative is resonating.
*   **Email is Working Harder:** Klaviyo saw a **127% surge in emails received** and a **44.5% jump in orders placed** via email, proving the list is active and buying.
*   **Google Ads Volume Up, Efficiency Down:** We spent 12.8% more to get 24% more impressions, but conversion rate dropped significantly (-72.9%). We need to tighten targeting to improve quality over quantity.
*   **Local Visibility is Healthy:** Google My Business saw a **20% increase in direction requests** and **18% more calls**, driving foot traffic to the Apothecary.

## **What Worked / What Didn’t**

**What Worked:**
*   **Scaling Meta Spend:** Increased budget directly correlated with massive reach and engagement gains.
*   **Email Frequency:** Sending more emails drove significantly more revenue, despite lower open rates.
*   **Video Content:** Instagram video metrics are leading the charge for organic growth.

**What Didn’t:**
*   **Google Ads Conversion Rate:** Traffic is there, but it's not converting as efficiently as previous months.
*   **GMB Website Clicks:** Fewer people clicking through to the site from Maps, though they are visiting in person.

---

## **Opportunities & Recommendations**

1.  **Refine Google Ads Targeting:** Review search terms and negative keywords to ensure the increased impressions are relevant. Test new ad copy to improve CTR and pre-qualify clicks.
2.  **Leverage Video on Meta:** Given the organic video success, let's push more video assets into the paid Meta campaigns to see if we can drive cheaper leads/engagement.
3.  **Segment Email Lists:** To combat the open rate drop, let's try segmenting the increased volume—sending highly targeted offers to active users and re-engagement campaigns to dormant ones.
