# OffGrid AI — Business Plan
### Local AI Installation & Setup Service

---

## The Problem You're Solving

South Africa has a unique intersection of challenges that make offline AI not just useful, but essential:

- **Load shedding** — Eskom outages can last 4–12 hours a day, cutting off cloud services entirely
- **Remote terrain** — The Drakensberg, Karoo, and rural Eastern Cape have zero reliable connectivity
- **Rural connectivity gaps** — Farming communities, schools, and clinics often lack broadband
- **Disaster risk** — Cape Town faces wildfire seasons; comms go down exactly when people need info most
- **Data costs** — Running cloud AI is expensive on SA mobile data rates (some of the priciest globally)

**Offline AI on a device sidesteps all of these problems.**

## What You're Actually Selling

You're not just selling software. You're selling **peace of mind, capability, and independence** from the grid and the internet.

## The Tech Stack

You don't need to build anything from scratch. You're a **systems integrator** — combining proven open-source tools:

### Core Engine
- **[Ollama](https://ollama.com)** — Runs LLMs locally with a dead-simple API. Supports Mac, Windows, Linux.
- **Models to offer:** Mistral 7B, Phi-3 Mini, Llama 3.2, Qwen2.5 (great multilingual support for Afrikaans/Zulu/Xhosa)

### Custom UI (Your "Reskin")
- **[Open WebUI](https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui)** — ChatGPT-style frontend that connects to Ollama. You brand it as your own.
- Or build a lightweight custom frontend with your OffGrid AI branding
- Lock it to your domain/splash screen so it feels like a product, not a DIY setup

### Hardware Options
- **Laptop install** — Any modern laptop with 8GB+ RAM can run Phi-3 or Mistral
- **Mini PC** — Beelink, MINISFORUM, or similar (R3,000–R6,000) pre-loaded and sold as a package
- **Raspberry Pi 5** — Budget option for very small models, good for schools
- **High-end rig** — For clients who want to run larger models (16B+), an RTX 3060+ GPU machine

## Your Unique Angle: Cape Town-First

Lead with the local story. Cape Town customers respond to:

- **"Works during loadshedding"** — This alone sells it
- **"No data needed"** — Huge on mobile-first users
- **"Runs on your own device, your data stays private"** — Appeals to privacy-conscious users and businesses
- **"Helps in the Cederberg, Hout Bay, or Hermanus"** — Geographic specificity builds trust

Consider targeting these Cape Town niches first:
- **Wine farms** — Need AI for admin, marketing copy, but have patchy internet
- **Tourism operators** — Guides, booking admins, content creation offline
- **Schools in under-served areas** — Township and rural schools with no broadband
- **Healthcare workers** — Community health workers in informal settlements

## Go-To-Market Strategy

### Phase 1 — Friends & Portfolio (Month 1–2)
- Do 3–5 installs for free or at cost for people you know
- Document everything with before/after photos and testimonials
- Build your case studies

### Phase 2 — Local Hustle (Month 2–4)
- Attend Cape Town tech meetups (Silicon Cape events, etc.)
- Post in Cape Town Facebook groups, Reddit (r/capetown), and community WhatsApp groups
- Offer a free "loadshedding readiness audit" as a lead magnet

### Phase 3 — Productize (Month 4–6)
- Launch a simple website (OffGrid AI or similar)
- List pre-configured devices on Takealot or Gumtree
- Partner with a local PC shop for referrals

## Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Hardware costs too high | Start with software-only installs on client's existing devices |
| Models too slow on cheap hardware | Be upfront about requirements; demo before selling |
| Clients don't understand AI | Offer a 30-min hands-on training session with every install |
| Competition from cloud AI going cheaper | Position on privacy + offline capability, not price |
| Open-source models improving fast | This is actually good — your product gets better for free |
